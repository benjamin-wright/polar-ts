# polar-ts — Story & Premise

## The Setup

Our protagonist is a young **polar bear cub** who wakes one morning to discover
that a gang of **penguins** has stolen their beloved **teddy bear**. The cub's
parents are deep in hibernation and can't be roused — so the cub takes it upon
themself to get the teddy back.

## The Journey

The penguin gang has scattered across the Arctic, and the teddy is nowhere to be
found. The cub treks from place to place following clues to where the penguins
went:

- **Search for clues** — question locals, poke around camps, and piece together
  the gang's trail.
- **Recruit friends** — other Arctic animals join the cub's party along the way,
  each lending their own strengths to the quest.
- **Do odd jobs** — earn favors and resources by helping out the folks you meet.
- **Upgrade the boat** — trade the proceeds of those odd jobs for boat upgrades
  that open up new, previously unreachable corners of the Arctic.

## Conflict Without Fighting

Nobody throws a punch in this game. Instead, the roaming members of the penguin
gang must be **persuaded** — talked down, won over, or turned into allies —
through a turn-based debate system that apes traditional RPG combat. The cub and
their friends defend their _self-esteem_ rather than hit points, and whittle
down an opponent's _anger_ rather than their health.

See [combat.md](./combat.md) for the full design of the persuasion system.
